Model

Applies to the layer selected above.
Loss = free-space at 1 m + 10·n·log10(distance) + wall losses + equipment-zone losses. Each wall crossing is counted once (touching walls and openings are merged). In a big open hall, n around 2 to 2.5 is typical.
Loss = 10·α·log10(d) + β + 10·γ·log10(f GHz), median value. Walls and equipment are not added: the coefficients already include typical obstructions on the same floor. Valid for 2 to 102 m (LOS) and 3 to 110 m (NLOS); outside that the result is extrapolated. Uncertainty σ is 2.6 dB (LOS) or 5.7 dB (NLOS). Meant for indoor links, not for towers outside the building.
